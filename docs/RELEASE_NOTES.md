# Questline 3.9.0 Release Notes

Questline 3.9 strengthens the behavioral loop between Habit planning, daily execution, reconciliation, and motivational Review.

## Habit rhythm

- Habits separate the period target, planned opportunities, and actual outcomes.
- High-frequency Habits use a weekly day strip for fast retrospective reconciliation.
- Complete, skip, plan, clear, and batch actions save immediately.
- At least three future opportunities remain visible.
- Monthly Habits support fixed day, ordinal weekday, and manual-per-cycle recurrence.
- Stable recurrence identities reduce duplicate generated occurrences.
- Moving or editing an occurrence returns to the same Habit cycle context.
- Habit status is derived as On Pace, Catch Up, At Risk, or Achieved.

## Positive Review

- Review opens with life areas.
- The first view recognizes activities closed, important moves, recoveries, Habit targets, active days, pace, and areas with momentum.
- Big Moves and Recovery Wins appear ahead of raw completion volume.
- Life-area evidence includes Tasks, Projects, Explorations, Habits, inactivity, and important neglected work.
- Attention Footprint describes recorded movement without claiming untracked actual time.
- Quiet by Choice remains available for intentionally dormant areas.

## Visual system

- Positive-behavior recognition and metric assets are integrated.
- Life-area selection uses compact approved icons instead of a separate primary-area dropdown.
- One primary and up to two shared areas remain supported.
- Dynamic text, counts, and controls remain responsive HTML rather than baked-in image text.

## Compatibility

- Version: `3.9.0`
- Schema: `21`
- Storage key: `questline-v3-9`
- Version 3.8 and earlier records are included in the migration path.
