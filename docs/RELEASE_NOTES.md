# Questline 3.8.0 Release Notes

Questline 3.8 makes recurring behavior and open-ended investigation substantially easier to operate from a phone.

## Habit-cycle reconciliation

- High-frequency Habits use a weekly day strip instead of a permanent batch form.
- Completions can be recorded retrospectively on the day they actually happened.
- Planned opportunities can be completed, skipped, returned to plan, moved, or removed.
- Moving an occurrence returns to the same Habit review and preserves context.
- Questline automatically matches actual completions to nearby open opportunities.
- Accidentally duplicated generated occurrences are consolidated during migration.
- Low-frequency and numeric Habits receive frequency-appropriate review flows.
- The next three upcoming opportunities remain visible.

## Actionable Exploration

- Explorations open around a visible Next Move.
- Questions, Experiments, and Options become compact investigation threads.
- Every investigation thread can generate multiple concrete Tasks inline.
- Linked Tasks retain source context and appear normally in Today.
- Supporting Ideas, Evidence, and Decisions remain compact and actionable.

## Review and life-area insight

- Review opens by life area.
- High-priority movement is recognized separately from raw Task volume.
- New cross-cutting insights surface priority misalignment, repeated replanning, dormant areas, and maintenance without advancement.
- Attention Footprint remains evidence based and does not claim untracked actual time.

## Priority readability

- Rank appears in one fixed compact location on Task and Habit cards.
- Priority ordering is rank-first; Stars remain a visible emphasis and tie-breaker rather than silently overriding rank.
- Resolve lanes are ordered by Priority Rank.

## Data and compatibility

- Version `3.8.0`
- Schema `20`
- Storage key `questline-v3-8`
- Version 3.7 and earlier data is migrated and backed up locally.
