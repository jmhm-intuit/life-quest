# Known Limitations

- Review uses completed Activities, progress records, Habit results, and Quest movement. It does not claim actual elapsed time without time tracking.
- Quiet by Choice currently uses a 30-day default.
- Habit batch reconciliation applies the displayed occurrence date unless the actual date is changed first.
- Exploration Decision readiness is derived from available questions, Tasks, evidence, and decisions; it is intentionally qualitative.
- Secondary life areas are limited to two.
- Direct calendar editing remains outside this release; imported calendar commitments can be hidden locally.
